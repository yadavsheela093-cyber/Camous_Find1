    How it works

    1. 1

      Post a ticket

      Say what you lost or found, where, and when. Add a photo if you have one.

    2. 2

      Search the board

      Filter by lost or found, category, or any word like a place or colour.

      Contact and return

      Open the ticket, get in touch, then mark it as returned so the board stays current.
